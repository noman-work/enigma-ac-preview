VRF Air Conditioning Installation

VRF (Variable Refrigerant Flow) runs many indoor units from shared outdoor units, sending each room only the refrigerant it needs.

It suits multi-floor offices, schools with lots of rooms, hotels and larger shops, where each room needs its own control. With some systems, one room can heat while another cools.

What a VRF installation involves
Outdoor unitsPlaced for service access, airflow and the neighbours
Refrigerant pipeworkBranch kits and insulated runs, routed neatly
Indoor unitsCassette, ducted or wall-mounted, chosen room by room
CommissioningPressure test, evacuation and set-up

Comfortable Cooling for Your Home

A top-floor flat or a south-facing bedroom can be hard to use on a hot day, and hard to sleep in at night.

A quiet wall-mounted unit, sized for the room, lets you set the temperature you want. We look at the room, the outside wall and the pipe route first, then recommend a system.

  • Flats and apartments. Split systems where there’s a suitable place for the outdoor unit.
  • Bedrooms and baby rooms. We talk you through unit position, noise and controls.
  • Several rooms. A multi-split system runs several indoor units from 1 outdoor unit.
