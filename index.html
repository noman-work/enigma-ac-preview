Information we still need before launch

  • Phone number and email. Call Now buttons are ready and will dial once the number is added.
  • F-Gas certificate and REFCOM registration number, so the credentials can be verified and the number shown.
  • Brand name. The supplied logo reads “Enigma Experimental Engineering”. Please confirm the public name and whether an air conditioning version of the logo exists.
  • Genuine reviews (the reviews section is left out until we have them), confirmed Kent and London towns, VRF scope and whether servicing is offered.
  • Photos of school, nursery and commercial kitchen jobs. No empty image frames are shown; these sections use our own site photos or text until real ones arrive.
  • Permission to publish project photographs and captions.
