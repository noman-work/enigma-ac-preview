VRF Air Conditioning Installation

VRF (Variable Refrigerant Flow) runs many indoor units from shared outdoor units, sending each room only the refrigerant it needs.

It suits multi-floor offices, schools with lots of rooms, hotels and larger shops, where each room needs its own control. With some systems, one room can heat while another cools.

  • Outdoor units placed for access and airflow
  • Branch pipework, insulated and neatly routed
  • Cassette, ducted or wall-mounted indoor units
  • Pressure testing, evacuation and commissioning
